# Shared BTCUSDT engineering pilot — 22 November 2026

Five consecutive, non-overlapping Binance Spot L2/trade recordings from one
morning: two train, one validation, two test. Each has 450 applied depth
updates: 2,250 in total, with 4,282 accepted public trades and no reported
depth reconnects or trade-ID gaps. Two buffered depth updates arrived before
their snapshots and were retained for book warmup only, leaving 2,248 feature
rows. Raw captures and machine-local reports are not committed.

## Project 2 — paired quotes

The fixed experiment used the 10-update logistic signal and one tick of
one-sided widening, with a 10 bps assumed maker fee and 1 ms simulated
order and cancel latency. Both arms used the same L2 replay settings.

| Session | Plain fills | Widened fills | Plain net P&L (USDT) | Widened net P&L (USDT) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Test 01 | 4 | 3 | -0.18732384 | -0.18731385 |
| Test 02 | 7 | 7 | -0.50702596 | -0.50702596 |

The model forecast up on 898 of 899 joined updates. Both policies lost
money; the observed difference was negligible. These consecutive sessions
test the workflow, not trading edge, execution realism on order-level data,
or performance across market regimes. Any model or quote settings changed
after this report need fresh test recordings.
